## 1. Scenario Overview
The insured, Mark T. Henderson, filed an online FNOL stating that on the evening of October 18, 2026 at approximately 08:30 PM, he was driving his silver 2003 Toyota Celica GT coupe westbound along Stevens Creek Boulevard in San Jose, CA. He claimed that an unidentified dark pickup truck aggressively cut into his lane from the right, forcing him to swerve and strike a concrete drainage culvert on the front passenger (right) side.

However, the responding California Highway Patrol collision report, an independent gas station surveillance witness, and the collision repair shop tell a contradictory story. The officer was dispatched at 11:45 PM to an accident scene on Highway 85 Northbound near Saratoga Ave, where the Celica was found lodged against a metal guardrail with severe **front-left (driver-side)** crush damage, bent tie rod, and collapsed wheel well. Furthermore, the VIN recorded by the investigating officer at the scene differs in its serial sequence from the insured's policy card, and the body shop found heavy oxidization/rust on structural radiator mounting points indicating substantial prior unrepaired collision damage.
